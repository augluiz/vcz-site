Commercial Intelligence

Structured analysis of the operation to identify where the business can grow — with more margin, more consistency and less noise.

  • Performance & Profitability
    Identifies where the business grows and where it truly earns. Volume variation, price trend, average ticket, frequency and active customers — connected to contribution margin and result levers by product, channel and customer.
  • Category Management
    Assortment, planogram and category tree optimization. Analysis of each item's strategic role — price leader, profitability, assortment filler, seasonal — and its role in building the ideal mix. Category share, shelf space and consumer profile.
  • Pricing & Competitiveness
    Price elasticity, price bands, competitiveness index and the impact on contribution margin from price list changes.
  • Trade Marketing
    Evaluation of in-store execution, shelf share, return on activation investment, promotional calendar and cooperative fund planning.
  • Commercial Logistics
    Out-of-stock rate, inventory coverage, turnover by product, order fill rate and logistics service level across the supply chain.
  • Payment Terms & Cycle
    Average collection period, operating financial cycle, commercial working capital and the impact of payment terms on profitability.
  • BI Development
    Structuring performance indicators, management dashboards and data storytelling to support decision-making in the operation.
  • Market Studies & Trends
    Analysis using Nielsen data: market share, penetration index and consumption trend mapping by category.
  • CRM & Loyalty
    Customer base segmentation by recency, frequency and value, identifying retention and reactivation opportunities.

Market Research

Study of the market, consumer and competition to validate strategies and identify opportunities before acting.

  • Brand Positioning
    Brand value analysis, consumer-perceived attributes, competitive positioning and identification of strategic gaps against the market.
  • Qualitative Research
    In-depth interviews, focus groups and ethnography to understand latent motivations, behavior drivers and adoption barriers.
  • Quantitative Research
    Surveys with representative samples, cluster segmentation and conjoint analysis to measure preferences and purchase intent.
  • Industry Benchmark

  • Consumer Behavior

  • Competitive Analysis
    Monitoring market share, competitive forces analysis, competitor positioning and detection of relevant strategic moves.
  • Strategic Intelligence & Scenarios
    Building forward-looking scenarios, macro-environment analysis, weak-signal mapping and strategic plan development based on market evidence.
